# Shot Templates — 短剧出海 CPS / Overseas Drama Promotion (Malaysia edition)
*New in v1.2. Sits ALONGSIDE shot-templates-novel-cn.md — the China track is unchanged.*

Goal: viewer watches the promo clip → clicks tracking link / searches app →
downloads → recharges to unlock episodes → you earn CPS commission on recharges
(rate varies by portal/tier — RS Boost shows it per promotion page).
Every creative decision serves the click-through.

## Two production modes (pick per project)
- **Mode A — Official clip re-cut:** download source clips ONLY from the CPS
  portal's material library (that IS your authorization). Claude's job: pick the
  paywall-adjacent cliffhanger, re-cut hook-first, add captions/subtitles, add CTA.
  No AI video generation cost. Never re-upload full episodes or rip from the app.
- **Mode B — AI original teaser (our pipeline's edge):** AI-generated
  anime/漫剧-style teaser dramatizing the drama's premise (AI 漫剧 is the
  fastest-growing segment). Check the portal's rules allow original promo
  material; label AI content per platform (TikTok AI label etc.).
- **Platform routing (2026):** TikTok → Mode B only (re-cut 搬运 clips get
  suppressed — TikTok now competes via Minis/PineDrama; original AI teasers
  ride the AI漫剧 wave). FB/YouTube → Mode A or B.

## Market presets (pick ONE per account and stay vertical)
- **EN-US女频:** billionaire/werewolf/revenge/contract-marriage; audience skews
  female 45–64; emotional-conflict captions. Post window: US evening.
- **CN-MY/SG:** 中文 audience in MY/SG/TW/HK on TikTok/YouTube; 战神归来/闪婚/
  重生 themes; captions 中文 + optional EN subtitle line.

Base style block (Mode B, per market):
- EN realistic: > vertical 9:16, cinematic live-action look, moody interior
  lighting, tense romantic-drama atmosphere, no watermarks
- 漫剧 style: > vertical 9:16, premium anime-drama style, cinematic lighting,
  expressive faces, high-contrast color grade, no watermarks

## D1 — Conflict cold-open (shot 1, ≤2s to impact)
[ACTION] the single most humiliating/shocking beat of the drama premise (she's
served divorce papers at her own gala / the janitor removes his cap — it's the
chairman). [CAMERA] fast push-in on the face. [AUDIO] one contradiction line:
"They threw her out with nothing. Tomorrow she owns the bank." Bass hit.

## D2 — Stakes in one image
[ACTION] one visual that explains the premise (the ring hits the floor / the
hidden ID card / the contract with a red seal). [CAMERA] insert close-up.
[AUDIO] second narration beat, tension riser.

## D3 — Escalation beats (repeat 2–3x)
[ACTION] one reversal per shot: slap intercepted, identity revealed, rival's face
drops. Cut BEFORE each payoff completes. [CAMERA] whip-pan / hard cut rhythm.
[AUDIO] narration escalates; heartbeat/glass-crack SFX accents.

## D4 — Paywall cliffhanger + CTA (final shot)
[ACTION] freeze at maximum suspense — the exact beat where the app's paywall sits
if known. [CAMERA] freeze-frame with slight zoom. [AUDIO+CAPTION]
EN: "Watch what she does next — full episode in the app 👉 link in bio."
中文: "接下来她的反击更狠——完整版看主页链接。"

## Attribution & compliance (this is where the money leaks if done wrong)
- Tracking link/code from the CPS portal goes in: TikTok bio (+ pinned comment),
  YouTube description + pinned comment, FB page link. Verify the link resolves in
  the target country BEFORE posting.
- RS Boost (cps.reelshort.com) signup is open — invitation codes are optional
  rate-boosters, not entry tickets. Other portals are distributor-gated: official
  channels/verified first-tier distributors ONLY. Anyone selling codes/授权/courses
  is a scam (CCTV-covered pattern). Never pay to join.
- Meta 2026: FB/IG affiliate posts MUST use the Partnership-Ads/branded-content
  tag — untagged UGC-style affiliate = "Deceptive Practice" (rejection + account
  penalty).
- Disclosures: TikTok AI label for Mode B; YouTube altered/synthetic toggle;
  respect each portal's watermark/branding requirements on clips.
- Log per video in published-log.md: platform, drama title, link ID, mode A/B —
  CPS attribution disputes are only winnable with records.
