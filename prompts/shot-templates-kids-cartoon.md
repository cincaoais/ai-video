# Shot Templates — Kids Cartoon (optional preset)

Base style block (paste into every shot):
> 3D animated cartoon, soft Pixar-like rendering, bright cheerful pastel palette,
> rounded child-safe shapes, gentle even lighting, G-rated preschool aesthetic

Character rule: always reference the sheet — "@Image1 is {name}, {2-line visual
description}. Keep {name}'s colors, proportions and face identical."

Hard content rules: slow pacing · no chase/threat/panic/yelling · nothing a child
could dangerously imitate · calm warm audio, no sudden loud SFX · one clear
educational takeaway per video.

## K1 — Gentle hook (shot 1)
[ACTION] {character} discovers something delightful (a glowing seed, a wobbling
tower of blocks) and reacts with big warm surprise. [CAMERA] static wide, then slow
push-in. [AUDIO] friendly narrator: "{curiosity question ≤8 words}?"; soft chime.

## K2 — Learn beat
[ACTION] {character} performs the lesson action once, slowly ({waters plant /
counts three apples touching each}). On-screen the key word/number appears large
and friendly. [CAMERA] static medium. [AUDIO] narrator states the fact simply;
gentle xylophone underneath.

## K3 — Try-and-wobble beat (safe "mistake")
[ACTION] {character} tries, gently wobbles/fumbles (nothing scary), tilts head,
tries again correctly. [CAMERA] static medium, tiny slow zoom on success. [AUDIO]
narrator: "Almost! Let's try again — {tip}." Soft supportive tone.

## K4 — Celebrate + recap
[ACTION] {character} claps/twirls once beside the completed thing; the key word
reappears. [CAMERA] slow pull-back to wide. [AUDIO] narrator recaps the takeaway
in one sentence; cheerful but calm outro melody.
