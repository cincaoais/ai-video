# Shot Templates — TikTok UGC / Trending (EN)

Prompt anatomy (every shot, every time):
**[STYLE] → [SUBJECT] → [ONE ACTION] → [CAMERA] → [SETTING/LIGHT] → [AUDIO]**

Base style block (paste into every shot):
> vertical 9:16 UGC smartphone footage, handheld with slight natural shake,
> realistic available lighting, authentic non-cinematic look, no watermarks

## T1 — Pattern-interrupt hook (shot 1)
[STYLE] base UGC block. [SUBJECT] {person: age/outfit/vibe — keep identical across
shots}. [ACTION] a physical interrupt in the first second: slams laptop shut / drops
the object / turns around mid-sentence, then holds up {prop with 3–5 word text}.
[CAMERA] selfie POV, arm's length, slight reframe mid-shot. [AUDIO] excited casual
line: "{hook line ≤10 words}"; real room tone.

## T2 — Receipt/proof shot
[STYLE] base UGC block. [SUBJECT] same person. [ACTION] points phone camera at a
screen/notebook showing {the proof}, finger taps the key number. [CAMERA] handheld
over-shoulder, quick punch-in on the number. [AUDIO] "{one-line reaction}", soft tap
SFX.

## T3 — Talking beat (story middle)
[STYLE] base UGC block. [SUBJECT] same person, new micro-location (couch/car/kitchen
— signals time passing). [ACTION] talks to camera with one hand gesture per sentence.
[CAMERA] static phone propped, subject slightly off-center. [AUDIO] dialogue
"{beat lines}", ambient continues.

## T4 — B-roll insert (use cheap/fast model variant)
[STYLE] base UGC block, no people. [ACTION] {object/scene doing one thing: coffee
pouring, notification popping, city timelapse}. [CAMERA] locked close-up. [AUDIO]
matching SFX only. 3–4s max.

## T5 — Loop/CTA outro
[STYLE] base UGC block. [SUBJECT] same person. [ACTION] delivers the payoff line,
then begins the exact motion from shot 1 (loop illusion). [CAMERA] selfie POV.
[AUDIO] "{payoff}; follow for part 2" — only promise part 2 if we'll make it.

## Filled example (shot 1)
> vertical 9:16 UGC smartphone footage, handheld with slight natural shake, realistic
> indoor lighting, authentic non-cinematic look. A woman in her 20s in a grey hoodie
> at a cluttered desk snaps her laptop shut, looks straight into the camera and holds
> up a sticky note reading "$0 → $500". Selfie POV at arm's length with a slight
> reframe. She says excitedly: "Nobody told me THIS about side income..." — real room
> tone, faint keyboard clatter.

Caption style: bold white, black outline, 3–5 words on screen at a time, synced to
speech. Hashtags: 3–5, one broad + two niche + one trend tag.
