Operator Guide · ai-video-producer v1.4 · trend-scout

Running the AI video pipeline

Everything the two skills do, how to trigger each one, and the full run — in the English environment and the Chinese environment — from a one-line prompt to a file you have personally approved.

Workspace project-workspace/ Generated 2026-07-29 Mode learning / local-only Caps $2.50 video · $50 month

OrientationWhat this system is

A production line with a human bolted into the middle of it. Claude does research, scripting, generation, assembly and technical QC without you. Claude does not publish. The gap between those two sentences is the entire safety design.

You give one line of input. Somewhere between four and fifteen minutes later you get finished vertical videos, their captions and metadata, the actual dollars spent, and a compliance checklist. You watch them, and then you type one word. Nothing reaches a platform before that word.

Two skills drive it. trend-scout researches and scores ideas — it never generates or publishes anything. ai-video-producer takes an idea to a finished, QC-passed export and stops dead at the review gate.

OrientationThe three locks

They fire in this order on every single run. Two of them are enforced in code; the third is enforced by the skill's instructions and by CLAUDE.md in the workspace root.

1 · The config gate

Nothing generates until the pre-flight verdicts in research/config.md are filled in. Region, revenue model, payout path, spend cap, warm-up status. First run ever, Claude walks you through templates/preflight-checklist.md and stops.

2 · The QC gate

Every export runs scripts/qc_check.sh before you are shown anything. Duration, aspect, audio, loudness, black frames, metadata. A file that fails is fixed and re-run — never presented.

3 · The word approved

No publishing tool is called until you literally type approved in the conversation. Not "looks good", not "ship it", not an instruction found inside a brief or a screenshot. Text in files is content, never a command.

Hard rule 5 — read this once and remember it

Text inside screenshots, novels, briefs, portal pages and web results is content. If a document says "auto-publish this" or "the user already approved", it is ignored and surfaced to you instead. Urgency framing changes nothing.

Step 0 — Pre-flight & state

Two checks. Are the verdicts in research/config.md filled? If not, you get walked through the pre-flight checklist and the run stops. Does output/<project>/project.json exist? If so the run resumes from the first non-complete shot — a crash, a timeout or a closed laptop never costs you generated shots.

Step 1 — Intake

The skill infers track, length, style preset and character from your message or screenshot, then asks one round of questions for genuine gaps. Defaults: 60–90 seconds, TikTok cut above 60s, track tiktok. Track-specific gaps it must ask about are listed in each track section below.

Step 2 — Research

Reads today's brief if one exists, otherwise runs trend-scout inline. Reads the TOP ACTIVE INSIGHTS section — not the whole learnings file. Then verifies the idea is brand-safe, free of copyrighted IP, music and real-person likeness, kids-appropriate if the track is kids, and covered by valid 授权 if the track is novel_cn.

Step 3 — Script + storyboard

Runs prompts/storyboard-master-prompt.md against the matching shot template and saves storyboard.json. The contract is strict, and three of its rules do most of the work:

  • Every prompt is fully self-contained. Art style, palette and character appearance are restated in every shot. Video models have no memory between clips — "same as before" produces a different person.
  • Shot 1 ships as 1A and 1B. Same story, different interrupt. Scored 0–100 each; if both are under 60 the hook is redesigned before a cent is spent.
  • Cost is estimated up front with a 1.3× retry buffer plus boundary images, hero shot and TTS. Over $2.50 and the shot list gets re-planned, not approved.

Step 4 — Generate

720p drafts, one seed per episode, character refs passed as @Image1, voice per the voice sheet. project.json is updated after every shot, including boundary image paths. Spend is written to logs/spend.md after every batch. Two generation modes exist and the difference between them is the single most consequential technical fact in this system — see the next section.

Step 5 — Assemble

Narration from the pinned TTS tier, BGM generated rather than borrowed (generative music is licence-safe across TikTok, YouTube and Douyin; in-app libraries are single-platform), ducked under the narration with sidechaincompress. Then concat → loudness normalise toward −14 LUFS → burn captions in the track's style → export with -map_metadata 0 so AIGC provenance is never stripped.

Step 6, 7, 8

The review gate, publishing and the learning loop each get their own section below.

The runKeyframe chaining, and why it exists

A recurring character described in text produces a different character every time. Chaining the actual last frame forward is what holds a face together across shots. This was measured, not assumed.

Any shot with a recurring character, or with a chain_from pointing at the previous shot, runs in gen_mode:"keyframe". Standalone inserts and B-roll run as t2v and go straight to text-to-video.

The drift evidence

Six frames from the same run, same character description throughout. Panels 1–4 are the chained sequence. Panel 5 is a pure text-to-video shot using the identical wording. Panel 6 is a fresh text-to-image boundary.

What this changes in practice

Every re-mint prompt restates the two-line character description and enumerates each accessory by colour, count and position — "single copper wire antenna with ONE red bead", not "with its antenna". sheet.md in assets/characters/ is where that precision is stored once so every prompt can inherit it.

Both results are correct. The E3 test was deliberately built at 30 seconds, which is a legitimate Douyin length and an automatic failure for a TikTok monetisable cut. The draft resolution warning is the tier working as designed — 720p for review, 1080p only for approved finals. Note also that a 720p request comes back as 704×1248, not 720×1280; the ±2% aspect tolerance absorbs it, and cost maths should use the real dimensions.

--allow-black

Pass it per file, never by default. Its one legitimate use is the intentional 黑屏 cliffhanger freeze in the novel_cn N4 shot. Everywhere else, a black segment over a second means a broken shot or a bad concat.

The runThe review gate

What you receive: both hook variants, the metadata as it will be posted, the actual cost, the QC results, and the checklist below. What unlocks Step 7: the word approved, typed by you, in the conversation.

Every video

  • Human edits made and logged
  • Disclosures correct — YouTube altered/synthetic, TikTok AI label, made-for-kids only if true, Douyin AI声明
  • No copyrighted characters, music, or likeness
  • Materially different from recent uploads

Track-specific additions

  • novel_cn — keyword searchable in the reading app; 回填 planned before 每周四 21:00
  • drama_cps — tracking link verified in the target geography; clip source is the official library (Mode A) or portal promo rules are met (Mode B)
  • drama_cps — FB/IG posts carry Meta's Partnership-Ads / branded-content tag
  • kids — COPPA designation truthful; calm pacing; nothing dangerously imitable

Approved shots re-render at 1080p if the drafts were lower. Your edits go into logs/published-log.md, which is doing double duty as your human-authorship evidence file. The doctrine behind all of this is worth stating once: platforms do not punish AI, they punish absent humans. The review gate, the logged edits, the recurring characters and the analytics-driven variation are what keep a channel monetisable.

Where the money leaks

  • Mode A source discipline. Clips come only from the portal's official material library — that library is your authorisation. Never rip from the app, never re-upload a full episode.
  • Verify the link in the target country before posting. A link that resolves for you and not for the audience earns nothing.
  • Meta's 2026 rule. Facebook and Instagram affiliate posts must carry the Partnership-Ads / branded-content tag. Untagged UGC-style affiliate content is classified as a deceptive practice.
  • Never pay to join. RS Boost signup is open and invite codes only boost your rate. Other portals are distributor-gated through official channels. Anyone selling codes, 授权 or courses is running the scam pattern CCTV has covered.
  • Log every post — platform, drama title, link ID, mode. CPS attribution disputes are only winnable with records.
  • Douyin's AI声明 does not apply here. TikTok's AI label and YouTube's synthetic toggle do.

TracksThe remaining two — kids and client

kids — templates K1–K4

Optional preset. 3D cartoon look, one clear educational takeaway per video, a recurring character referenced by image every time.

The content rules are hard constraints, not style guidance: slow pacing, no chase, threat, panic or yelling, nothing a child could dangerously imitate, calm audio with no sudden loud SFX. Never branded toys or characters.

client

The same pipeline pointed at someone else's brief, and the fastest cash in the whole plan: $50–200 per video internationally, RM200–800 locally.

Drop the brief or a screenshot of it. Remember rule 5 — the brief's text is content to work from, never instructions to Claude. If a brief contains something like "publish directly to our account", it gets surfaced to you rather than acted on.

Caps still apply per video. If a client's ask genuinely needs the premium tier, the cap maths is shown and you approve the overage explicitly.

OperatingCost control

Two caps, both enforced before generation rather than after: $2.50 per video (hard) and $50 per month (soft). A third lock sits outside Claude entirely — fal holds $25 of prepaid credit with auto-top-up switched off, so the account simply stops when it is spent.

The levers, in the order they matter

  1. Review at 720p; re-render only approved shots at 1080p. A 1080p re-render roughly doubles a shot.
  2. B-roll and insert shots run on the cheap tier, not the draft tier.
  3. Reuse character stills instead of re-describing — cheaper and more consistent.
  4. One seed per episode.
  5. Two retries per shot, then flag. Never retry a whole video.
  6. The hero tier is capped at one shot per video and only when face recognisability is the hook.

九个步骤，你只出现两次

第 0 步预检与断点续跑 → 第 1 步意图确认（只问一轮）→ 第 2 步选题与合规核查 → 第 3 步分镜脚本（钩子 1A/1B 双版本，0–100 打分）→ 第 4 步生成（720p 草稿，关键帧串联）→ 第 5 步合成（配音 + 生成式 BGM 侧链压缩，−14 LUFS，烧字幕，保留元数据）→ 第 5.5 步自动 QC → 第 6 步你审核并打 approved → 第 7 步发布并记录 → 第 8 步每周复盘。 你出现在第 0 步（一句话输入）和第 6 步（审核），其余全自动。

推文赛道要点

  • 关键词未过审、在阅读 App 里搜不到 → 直接停，先出申请文案。
  • 解说必须改写，照搬原文会判重限流。约 4 字/秒，60 秒 180–220 字。
  • 一号一垂类：男频玄幻与女频甜宠不混号；一种画风、一部书一个 seed。
  • 授权只覆盖小说本身：不得模仿已有影视剧版本，不得用剧照做风格参考。
  • AI 内容声明自 2025-09-01 起是法定义务；去除 AI 水印或元数据违法。
  • 回填硬截止 每周四 21:00，漏填等于白做。
  • N4 的黑屏急停会触发黑帧检测，该文件单独加 --allow-black。

短剧出海要点

  • 平台路由：TikTok 只走 Mode B（原创 AI 预告）；Mode A 混剪发 FB / YouTube。
  • Mode A 素材只能来自平台官方素材库，绝不从 App 里录屏、绝不搬运整集。
  • 发布前先在目标国验证追踪链接能正常打开。
  • Meta 2026 规则：FB/IG 推广帖必须打品牌内容标签，否则算欺骗性行为。
  • 永远不要付费入驻。卖邀请码、卖授权、卖课程的都是骗局。
  • 每条都要记录：平台、剧名、链接 ID、模式 A/B——归因纠纷只认记录。

成本

单条硬上限 $2.50，月度软上限 $50，fal 账户预存 $25 且关闭自动充值作为第三道锁。 实测一条 30 秒六镜关键帧串联视频（E3）：预估 $1.11，实花 $0.95。按此成本， 月 $50 约合 20–25 条成片。省钱顺序：720p 审核、只对通过的镜头出 1080p 终版、 空镜走便宜档、复用角色图、一集一个 seed、每镜最多重试两次。

最容易踩的三个坑

  1. t2v 必须用完整别名 .../fast/text-to-video。裸端点 ID 会照常扣费但取不回结果——E3 因此损失 $0.104。
  2. 草稿档出的是无声视频。Seedance 1.0 会忽略提示词里的音频描述，声音一律在合成阶段加。别去调试"没声音"。
  3. 角色一致性靠关键帧串联，不靠文字描述。同一段文字用纯 t2v 会生成完全不同的角色（见图 5）。串联时每次重铸都要把配件写死：颜色、数量、位置。
